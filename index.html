FAQ

Do the pools really merge?

No pool can be fused without the coins' own programs, so we don't pretend. A wedding is a public vote by both communities, and when it passes the smaller coin's holders move into the bigger coin with one real swap. One narrative, one runner.

Which coins can get tied?

Any two Solana tokens with a price, not just coins launched here. That's the point: vamps launch everywhere.

Who can vote?

Anyone holding either coin. Your weight is your share of that coin's supply, read from chain when you vote and again when the window closes.

What does it cost?

Signing in and voting are free signatures. Swaps and launches cost the normal network and pump.fun fees, nothing more.

Is this financial advice?

No. Memecoins are volatile. Read the coin before you move in.
