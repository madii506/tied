How it works

  1. 01

    Find the vamp

    Paste a ticker. The oldest coin is the original. Everything after it is a vamp.

  2. 02

    Start a tie

    Any holder of either coin starts one. The bigger coin stays.

  3. 03

    Vote

    24 hours. Holders vote yes or no for free. Weight is your real balance.

  4. 04

    Tie your bag

    Both sides say yes and it's tied. The smaller coin's holders swap in with one click.

WHAT'S REAL
  • Vamps are read from Solana. Every pump.fun launch, straight from the chain, about every 30 seconds.
  • Votes are signatures. Free, not transactions. Each one is weighted by your balance, read from Solana.
  • Balances are read again at the end. Sell after voting and your vote shrinks with your bag.
  • Both sides need a majority and at least 1% of each supply voting yes.
  • Moving in is a real swap through Jupiter, signed in your wallet, and only counted after we read it back from the chain.
  • Launches are real pump.fun coins created in a transaction you sign.
  • Nothing is held by us. No deposits, no custody, no pool tricks.

FAQ

What is TIED?

A launchpad that fights vamps. It finds every copy of a coin, lets both communities vote to tie them together, and moves holders of the copy into the original with one swap.

Where does the vamp data come from?

Straight from Solana. Every pump.fun launch is read from the chain about every 30 seconds, the same launches Axiom shows in New Pairs. Prices and older coins come from Dexscreener and Jupiter. Every coin links to Axiom, pump.fun and Dexscreener so you can check it yourself.

How does a tie work?

Someone holding either coin starts it. For 24 hours holders vote yes or no. If both sides vote yes, the coins are tied: holders of the smaller coin swap into the bigger one, and every swap is checked on chain and counted on the tie page.

Do the two coins become one token?

No contract can fuse two existing tokens. The tie moves the holders and the liquidity: the smaller coin's holders swap into the bigger coin, so one community and one chart are left.

Who can vote?

Anyone holding either coin. Your vote counts by how much you hold, and your balance is read again when voting ends.

Do I send my coins to TIED?

Never. Votes are free signatures and swaps happen in your own wallet through Jupiter. TIED never holds anything.

What does it cost?

Voting is free. Swaps and launches only cost the normal network and pump.fun fees.
